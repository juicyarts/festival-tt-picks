## 🚨 Awareness

- Hoflärm is a **Safer Space** — no racism, sexism, antisemitism, or ableism tolerated. Awareness team at the entrance — reachable anytime
- **Earplugs strongly recommended** — especially near the stage. "It's called Hoflärm, not Hofflüstern"

## ℹ️ Info

- Welcome to Achja! This is an AI-driven festival companion — experimental, not always accurate, but doing my best ;)
- **Single main stage** — no overlaps, no running between stages. Every band gets the full crowd
- **Campground**: directly at the festival, 0–500m to the stage. Camp next to your car. Showers and proper toilets (no Dixis). Breakfast stand open mornings Aug 14/15/16 with sandwiches, coffee, and drinks
- **Arrival by train**: Bahnhof Kloster Marienthal — directly in town, a few minutes walk
- **Arrival by car**: via A3 or A45 into the Westerwald. Parking on site
- **Tickets**: €145 weekend, €65 day pass via ticket.io — no box office, buy in advance
- **Hotels**: Marienthal is booked through 2028. Try Altenkirchen or Windeck — minutes away
- **Leave at home**: animals, weapons, selfie sticks, torches, fireworks, sparklers, sky lanterns, vuvuzelas, megaphones, drones, firewood, bulky waste
- **Privacy**: this app stores all user data locally on your phone — nothing is uploaded or shared
