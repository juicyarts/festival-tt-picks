## 🚂 Getting There

- **Train:** Bahnhof Kloster Marienthal — directly in town, a few minutes walk to the festival
- **Car:** Via A3 or A45 into the Westerwald. Parking directly on site

## 🏕 Camping

- Campground directly at the festival (0–500m to stage)
- Camp next to your car — no separate parking
- Showers and proper toilets available (no Dixis)
- Breakfast stand open mornings Aug 14/15/16 — sandwiches, coffee, drinks
